About

A game about one thing: cutting it exactly in half.

Hold, slide and release to bring the knife down. HALF! measures both pieces by their real 3D volume, so a PERFECT really is 50.00% to 50.00%. Within one point is PERFECT, within five is GREAT, and past the limit the stage starts over.

  • 51 things to cut in six sets, from tomatoes to a diamond, a soccer ball and a bar of soap.
  • Each item stops somewhere new on the belt, so every cut needs a fresh aim.
  • Twist stages change the rules: No Guide hides the cut line, Rush keeps the belt moving, Wobble rocks the item.
  • Eight blades and seven kitchens to unlock, collection sets, chef ranks, daily quests and a Daily Cut.
  • Short sessions, no timers or energy, plays offline.
