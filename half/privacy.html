HALF! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

HALF! is a single-player mobile game published by Min_27.

Data collection

HALF! does not require an account, and Min_27 does not collect, sell, or receive personal information. Starting with version 0.2.0, the game includes advertising and in-app purchases provided by the third parties described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

HALF! shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, cap how often they appear, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process device information. This includes the device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and some US states), HALF! asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options button in the game. On iOS, HALF! asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before using the advertising identifier.

For details on how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Rewarded ads are always optional. The paid "Remove Ads" option turns off the ads that appear between stages.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Min_27 does not receive your payment information. The game keeps only a record on your device that the "Remove Ads" option was purchased, and the store can restore this record.

Data stored on the device

HALF! stores game progress and preferences locally on the player's device, such as stage progress, unlocked items, virtual currency, daily quests, and audio settings. This information is used only to run the game and is not sent to Min_27. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children

HALF! is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the HALF! store listing.
